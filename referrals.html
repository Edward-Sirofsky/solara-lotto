How referral rewards work

The prize pool is never touched. Every ticket still puts 100% of its price into the round on-chain, and 80% of every pool still goes to players. Referral rewards come out of the 20% operator fee, not out of the prizes.

A referred ticket pays its referrer 5% of the ticket price. Referrals are attributed on-chain: a referred purchase carries the referrer's wallet in a memo on the same transaction that buys the tickets, so every figure below traces back to a real transaction. Rewards are paid in SOL after each weekly draw.

Only approved referrers earn. Self-referral does not count.
