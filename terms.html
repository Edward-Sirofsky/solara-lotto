5.How the Lottery Works

  • Tickets. You purchase a ticket by paying the ticket price in SOL through a connected wallet. Each ticket is assigned a random 6-digit number.
  • Draws. A winning number is drawn on a recurring schedule (currently weekly). The winning number is generated from verifiable randomness (VRF) supplied by MagicBlock's VRF oracle; the randomness proof is verified on-chain by MagicBlock's VRF program before the lottery program uses the result.
  • Prizes. Prizes are awarded by matching digits of your ticket against the winning number, from left to right, across defined prize brackets. A portion of the total pool is allocated to each bracket.
  • Operator fee. An operator fee (currently 20% of the pool) is applied to fund draws and operations. The fee is subject to change; the current fee is shown in the interface.
  • Claiming. Prizes are not paid automatically. If you win, you must claim your prize by submitting a signed transaction from the winning wallet. Prizes remain claimable while the relevant round is open for claims.
  • Rollover. Prize amounts in a bracket that are not won or not claimed may roll over into a future round's pool, as determined by the on-chain program.

The parameters above reflect the current configuration of the protocol and may be changed. The on-chain program is the authoritative source of truth for all ticket, draw, and payout logic.
