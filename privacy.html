Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operators of SOLARA ("SOLARA", "we", "us", or "our") handle information in connection with the SOLARA website, mobile application, and related interfaces (the "Service") to the decentralized, on-chain lottery protocol on the Solana blockchain. SOLARA is non-custodial and account-free: we do not require you to register, and we do not collect your name, email, or private keys to use the Service.

1.Our Approach to Privacy

We designed SOLARA to collect as little information about you as possible. You interact with the protocol directly through your own wallet. We do not operate user accounts, we do not hold your funds, and we never have access to your private keys or seed phrase. Most of what happens takes place on the public Solana blockchain, which we neither control nor own.

2.Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or ask for your name, email address (unless you choose to contact us), postal address, government identifiers, passwords, private keys, or seed phrases. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

3.Information Involved When You Use the Service

  • Blockchain data. When you buy a ticket, claim a prize, or otherwise transact, your public wallet address and transaction details are recorded on the Solana blockchain. This data is public, permanent, and outside our control — it is a property of the blockchain, not something we collect or can delete.
  • Wallet connection. When you connect a wallet, the interface reads your public wallet address to display your tickets and results. We do not store this off-chain in a way that identifies you personally.
  • Technical and usage data. Like most websites and apps, our interfaces and infrastructure may automatically process limited technical data such as IP address, device or browser type, app version, and interaction logs. This is used for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and to keep the Service running.
  • Local storage. The Service may store non-identifying preferences (such as display settings) locally on your device. This data stays on your device.

4.How We Use Information

To the extent we process any technical data, we use it only to: operate, maintain, and improve the Service; diagnose and fix problems; protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use it to advertise to you or to sell to third parties.

5.Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Service uses minimal cookies or local-storage mechanisms that are necessary for it to function (for example, remembering your preferences or maintaining a session). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can control or clear local storage and cookies through your browser or device settings, though some features may not work correctly if you do.

6.Third-Party Services

The Service depends on third parties that may process technical data under their own privacy policies, including:

  • Wallet providers you choose to connect;
  • RPC and blockchain-infrastructure providers that relay your requests to the Solana network;
  • Hosting and content-delivery providers that serve the website and application;
  • App distribution platforms through which you may install the mobile application.

We do not control these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies. Your use of a connected wallet or the underlying network is governed by that provider's terms and privacy practices, not ours.

7.Blockchain Data Is Public and Permanent

Because SOLARA operates on a public blockchain, any transaction you make is visible to anyone and cannot be altered or erased by us or by you. Wallet addresses are pseudonymous but may be linked to your identity by third parties through external analysis. Please keep this in mind before transacting.

8.Data Retention

We retain limited technical and log data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized. On-chain data persists on the blockchain indefinitely and is not subject to deletion by us.

9.Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the infrastructure behind the Service. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and the security of your wallet and keys is your responsibility. We cannot guarantee absolute security and are not liable for unauthorized access beyond our control.

10.Children

The Service is not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under 18 years of age (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has used the Service, please contact us.

11.International Users

The Service may be accessed from around the world, and any technical data processed may be handled in jurisdictions other than your own. By using the Service, you understand that data may be processed in locations with different data-protection laws than those of your country.

12.Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data that we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we collect very little personal data and do not maintain accounts, we may have no personal data to act on. Where we do, you can contact us to make a request. Note that on-chain data cannot be changed or deleted by us.

13.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

14.Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, contact us at solaralotto@gmail.com.
